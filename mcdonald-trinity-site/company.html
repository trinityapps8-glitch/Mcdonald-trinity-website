Products, shipped

What the team has built.

Three products carry the Emtrixz name into the world — each designed, built and shipped by the studio's young Ugandan developers.

Mentorship

MFL Mentorship App

Built for Music For Life, an organisation educating children across Uganda — connects students with mentors so both can track progress and grow together.

Emotional wellness · Feel · Grow · Achieve

Encourage Me

Daily check-ins, encouragement content — songs, videos, quotes, meditations — an emotion journey tracker, and a private, safe space to build confidence.

Education · Learn · Grow · Succeed Together

Learn Together

An online learning management system for any institution — courses, assignments, communication, resources, progress analytics and mentorship in one platform.

Communication

Vidtrixz

Video calls, reimagined — the studio's own communication product, built end to end in-house.

Institutional management

Stoplight Registration System

Built to register and track the institutions taking part in Stoplight — keeping enrolment, participation and progress organised in one place.

Why Emtrixz exists

Real problems, given to young people.

Trinity's approach traces straight back to his own run through the Stanbic National Schools Championship — the same national innovation programme, not a sport, that first put him in front of judges defending a working prototype. Emtrixz is that same idea, scaled into a company: hand young Ugandans a real problem, real clients, and real responsibility, and they'll rise to meet it.

About Trinity

A leader who builds things — and builds people.

McDonald Trinity is a young, sharp-minded entrepreneur out of Kampala with a rare combination of technical vision and genuine people leadership. As Founder and CEO of Emtrixz Technology, he's spent years turning ideas into working software — while quietly doing something more important: bringing teenagers and young Ugandans into the room, teaching them to code, ship, and lead.

That instinct for leadership was tested on a national stage at the Stanbic National Schools Championship — not a sports competition, but Uganda's flagship school innovation and entrepreneurship programme, where student teams design and pitch real solutions to real problems in front of judges.

Correcting the record

The Stanbic National Schools Championship is an innovation competition — not a sport.

It's easy to assume "championship" means a trophy on a pitch. It doesn't. It's Uganda's largest school-level innovation and entrepreneurship programme, run by Stanbic Bank as part of its corporate social investment, and it has reached close to a million learners since 2015.

What actually happens: student teams identify a real community problem, design a working prototype or business idea, get mentored through a competitive regional process, and — for the strongest teams — present their prototype at a national residential bootcamp before a grand finale in front of judges and industry mentors.
